# TMUG — Approved Brand Content & Copywriting Guidelines

---

## 1. Brand Hook & Primary Positioning

### Primary Brand Hook

Secondary Tagline:
> *"Tea, but make it TMUG."*

---

## 2. Voice & Tone Principles

- **Warm**: Hospitable, embracing, rooted in everyday Indian chai pauses and hospitality.
- **Playful**: Witty without being trivial; approachable, vibrant, and energetic.
- **Premium**: Crafted with care, editorial precision, and aesthetic discipline.
- **Conversational**: Speaks like a tea-loving friend sharing an exceptional brew — no stiff corporate jargon.
- **Modern**: Forward-thinking, direct-to-consumer clarity designed for young, discerning palates.
- **Indian**: Proudly celebrating local rituals — kulhad cups, morning kadak chai, adrakwali memories, Darjeeling estate lore, and blue aparajita petals.
- **Concise**: Clear, rhythmic sentences that respect user attention and drive conversion.
