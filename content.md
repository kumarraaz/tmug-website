# TMUG — Approved Brand Content & Copywriting Guidelines

This document specifies the official brand narrative, approved headlines, voice and tone standards, and verified product copy across the TMUG storefront.

---

## 1. Brand Hook & Primary Positioning

### Primary Brand Hook
> **"Tea, but make it fun."**

Secondary Tagline:
> *"Tea, but make it TMUG."*

---

## 2. Voice & Tone Principles

- **Warm**: Hospitable, embracing, rooted in everyday Indian chai pauses and hospitality.
- **Playful**: Witty without being trivial; approachable, vibrant, and energetic.
- **Premium**: Crafted with care, editorial precision, and aesthetic discipline.
- **Conversational**: Speaks like a tea-loving friend sharing an exceptional brew — no stiff corporate jargon.
- **Modern**: Forward-thinking, direct-to-consumer clarity designed for young, discerning palates.
- **Indian**: Proudly celebrating local rituals — kulhad cups, morning kadak chai, adrakwali memories, Darjeeling estate lore, and blue aparajita petals.
- **Concise**: Clear, rhythmic sentences that respect user attention and drive conversion.

---

## 3. Section Copy & Messaging Standards

### Hero Section
- **Main Headline**: *"Tea, but make it fun."*
- **Supporting Copy**: *"Whole-flower herbal infusions that bloom blue and ruby, single-origin Darjeeling green leaves, and unapologetically kadak Assam CTC chai — packed fresh for your everyday ritual."*
- **Primary Action (CTA)**: `"Explore Our Teas"` (jumps to `#shop`)
- **Secondary Action**: `"Official Amazon Store"` (links to verified Amazon store)
- **Verified Claims Only**: 100% natural, no synthetic essences, direct garden sourcing.

### Shop Collections
- **Section Heading**: *"Shop our collections"*
- **Subtitle**: *"From slow caffeine-free evening flowers to proper morning doodh chai."*
- **Approved Active Categories**:
  - `All Teas` — The full lineup of whole flower teas, green tea, and chai blends.
  - `Flower Teas` — Whole dried butterfly pea, chamomile, and hibiscus blooms.
  - `Chai` — Strong CTC chai patti for authentic kadak milk tea (Premium and Gold).
  - `Green Tea` — Whole loose-leaf unfermented tea from high-altitude Darjeeling gardens.
  - `Herbal & Fresh` — Pure citrus lemongrass leaves.
  - `Bestsellers` — Verified customer favorite blends.

> [!NOTE]
> Only categories that exist in `src/data/collections.ts` are approved for display.

### Why TMUG Section
- **Main Headline**: *"Tea should feel exciting, not ordinary."*
- **Subtitle**: *"Most supermarket tea is dusty CTC sweepings or artificial flavor sprays. We set out to change that."*
- **Approved Differentiators**:
  - **Whole Flowers & Leaves**: Whole dried petals and whole loose leaves that retain natural essential oils.
  - **Clean & Pesticide-Tested**: Sourced responsibly without synthetic colorants or chemical sprays.
  - **Kadak Without Compromise**: Full-bodied Assam CTC selected specifically to hold strength against milk and fresh ginger.
  - **Fresh Batch Packaging**: Sealed in airtight UV-protective jars and pouches to protect botanical aromatics.

### Made With Real Tea Section
- **Main Headline**: *"Real leaves. Real flowers. Real flavour."*
- **Supporting Copy**: *"Turn the pack over. Every ingredient is a plant you recognize."*
- **Verified Botanical Transparency**:
  - **Aparajita (Butterfly Pea)**: *100% dried Clitoria ternatea flowers.*
  - **Babune ke Phool (Chamomile)**: *100% dried Matricaria chamomilla blossoms.*
  - **Gudhal (Hibiscus)**: *100% dried Hibiscus sabdariffa petals.*
  - **Nimbu Ghas (Lemongrass)**: *100% dried Cymbopogon leaves.*
  - **Darjeeling Loose Leaf**: *100% unfermented whole-leaf green tea.*
  - **Assam CTC**: *100% black tea granules from select Assam gardens.*

## 4. Retail & Marketplace Verification

## 5. Reviews & Social Proof Integrity

- **Customer Reviews**: Use only verified quotes from actual purchasers (documented in `src/data/reviews.ts`).
- **Rating Integrity**: Never invent arbitrary review counters or fake star counts.
- **Press Coverage**: Feature press quotes only when formal editorial coverage is verified and approved.
